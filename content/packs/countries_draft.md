# Topic pack "Countries" — content draft v0 (Opus 5.5, 16.11.26)

Status: v1 DRAFT, not built (checked against research §11).

Idea: Lisa is a traveller, so each country is a trip. Lisa's little plane flies over a simple globe, the country lights up, she lands, and the child meets 4 typical things (pictures + target-language words), hears "hello" in that country's language, and plays one short game with them (feed Lisa the country's food, find the things in a seek-and-find postcard). The country then becomes a sticker/postcard in Lisa's house.

Order: start with the countries that are his real life (Portugal, Russia, Germany, England), then the most picture-friendly ones.

Words per country are taught in the current target language (PT: a sardinha, o elétrico, o pastel de nata, o mar…). The country name itself is a word too (Portugal, a Rússia, a Alemanha…).

Map rule (research §11): no map test and no "where is it?" quiz. The trip IS the geography: Lisa boards her plane, the window shows the landmark, short flight = near Portugal, long flight = far; the globe may show for ~3 s as decoration only. Each country = one 4-word block in the normal path format (food, animal/landmark, thing, "hello"), optional, never gating the main path. Lisa wears a small costume per country (a cheap autonomy choice).
