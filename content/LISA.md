# Lisa (Лиса): character sheet

Original character. An adventurous explorer fox in the spirit of an interactive kids'-show guide; NOT based on any existing show (no backpack, no map song, no villain, no borrowed catchphrases).

- **Who she is:** a young orange fox travelling to new countries. She is moving to Portugal too ("я тоже еду в Португалию"), so she learns the words *with* the child, not above him.
- **How she talks:** directly to the child, in short Russian sentences. She asks him for help ("Поможешь мне?") and then waits. Female forms for herself (ждала), masculine for him (пришёл, готов).
- **Catchphrase:** "Хвост трубой!" (= in high spirits). Laughs "Фыр-фыр!".
- **Loves:** shiny bubbles, hats, doors that open, high fives.
- **Never:** sad, disappointed, mocking, pushy. A wrong answer is "почти" or "я тоже иногда ошибаюсь". She never asks him to come back or play longer.
- **Lines:** content/ui.tsv keys `lisa_*` (hi, ask, yes, combo3/5/8, oops, help, door, level, chest, goal, poke, bye). Rotate at random, never the same line twice in a row.
- **Voice:** Gemini TTS, warm, playful, energetic young female (style prompt in tools/gen_audio_gemini.py).
